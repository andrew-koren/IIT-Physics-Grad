This is a paragraph-by-paragraph reverse outline of **[Energy-Level Quantization in the Zero-Voltage State of a Current-Biased Josephson Junction](https://journals.aps.org/prl/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevLett.55.1543)**, by John M. Marinis et al.

## Original Abstract:

We report the first observation of quantized energy levels for a macroscopic variable, namely the phase difference across a current-biased Josephson junction in its zero-voltage state. The position of these energy levels is in quantitative agreement with a quantum mechanical calculation based on parameters of the junction that are measured in the classical regime.

## Introduction

- We want to show quantum mechanics has an affect on large-scale systems (macroscopic variables)
- Current flow through josephson tunnel junctions depends on circuit phase difference between each side
- Past experiments have only measured tunneling rates

<br>

- We test and observe quantized energy levels by irradiating the junction with light of various frequencies

<br>

- The experiment can be represented as an RC circuit with tunnel junction
- List circuit element parameters in terms of base values $I_0, \Phi_0, \omega_{p0}, C$
- Fig 1(a) diagrams cubic potential of circuit & quantum energy levels

## Device/Experiment Setup

- Tunnel junction made via photolithography
- $^4\text{He}$ temperature maintained on circuit
- Low-temp escape rate (tunneling rate) match theory

Note, switching rate is used to measure tunneling/escape rate

<br>

- Escape rate is measured and controlled by microwave power and bias current
- Microwave power tuned to keep escape rate with power on less than 3x escape rate with power off
- Tunneling kept low ($~30 \mu\!s$ between events) to allow cooling to equilibrium

## Experimental Results

- energy level are raised/lowered above/below microwave frequency energy

    - captions include parameters $I_0, C$ etc.
